Privacy Policy for Pachira Wallet

Last updated: September 28, 2026

CEglobal ("we", "us", or "our") provides the Pachira Wallet mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Pachira Wallet.

1. Overview

Pachira Wallet is designed to help users organize and access membership cards, barcodes, QR codes, coupons, vouchers, reminders, and related information.

Pachira Wallet does not require users to create an account or register with CEglobal to use its main features.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Information that you enter into Pachira Wallet, such as membership card information, barcodes, QR codes, coupon information, notes, images, and reminders, may be stored locally on your device.

CEglobal does not operate a server for the purpose of collecting the contents of membership cards, coupons, vouchers, notes, or other information that users manually store in the app.

3. Camera and Images

Pachira Wallet may request access to your device camera when you choose to scan barcodes or QR codes.

The app may also allow you to select or use images for features such as cards, coupons, or vouchers. These permissions are used only to provide the features requested by the user.

4. Notifications and Reminders

Pachira Wallet may request permission to send notifications so that it can provide reminders created or configured by the user.

Notification permissions can be managed through your device settings.

5. Advertising

Pachira Wallet uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information, including device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, app interaction information, and diagnostic information, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

For more information about how Google handles information from apps that use its services, please visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

6. Firebase and Analytics

Pachira Wallet may use Google Firebase services to support app functionality, analytics, diagnostics, performance monitoring, or service improvement. Depending on the Firebase services enabled in the app, Google may process technical information such as device information, app interaction data, identifiers, crash information, and diagnostic data.

For more information about Firebase privacy and data processing, please visit:

Firebase Privacy and Security

7. Third-Party Services

Pachira Wallet may use third-party services that process information according to their own privacy policies. These services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase
  • Google Play Services

CEglobal does not control the independent privacy practices of these third-party service providers.

8. Data Sharing

CEglobal does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers, such as Google, when necessary to provide advertising, analytics, diagnostics, security, or other app-related services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through Pachira Wallet. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for their own devices and for information stored locally on those devices.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Pachira Wallet generally remains on the user's device until the user deletes the relevant information, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the app.

Data processed independently by third-party services is subject to the retention and deletion policies of those service providers.

11. Children's Privacy

Pachira Wallet is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a CEglobal account system because Pachira Wallet does not require users to create a CEglobal account to use its main features.

12. International Users

Pachira Wallet is available to users in multiple countries. Third-party service providers used by the app may process information in countries other than the country in which the user resides, subject to their respective privacy policies and applicable laws.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Pachira Wallet's features, services, or legal requirements change. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
