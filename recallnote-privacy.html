RecallNote Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

RecallNote is a study and note-taking application provided by CEglobal. We respect your privacy and design RecallNote to keep your study materials under your control.

1. Personal Information

RecallNote does not require you to create a CEglobal account. CEglobal does not directly collect your name, email address, or the contents of your notes, flashcards, or PDF annotations through the app.

2. Storage of Study Materials

Your study materials are stored on your device. If you choose to use synchronization, copies of the relevant data may be stored in the cloud service associated with your device or account.

3. iCloud Synchronization (Apple Devices)

On supported iPhone, iPad, and Mac devices, RecallNote can synchronize study data through Apple's iCloud service. This synchronization is managed using your Apple account and Apple's infrastructure. CEglobal does not operate a separate server to store your synchronized study materials.

4. Google Drive Synchronization (Android and Windows)

On supported Android and Windows devices, RecallNote can synchronize study data using your Google Drive account. This is separate from iCloud synchronization. Data stored in Google Drive is subject to Google's applicable privacy terms and account settings.

5. Purchases

Optional in-app purchases and subscriptions, where offered, are processed by the relevant platform store, such as Apple's App Store. CEglobal does not receive your full payment card details. The store may process transaction information according to its own privacy policy.

6. Third-Party Services

If you use iCloud, Google Drive, or a platform store, those services may process information necessary to provide their functionality. Please consult their respective privacy policies for details.

7. Data Control and Deletion

You can manage or delete your study materials within RecallNote, subject to the features available on your device. Cloud copies, if any, may also need to be managed through your iCloud or Google Drive account. Deleting the app alone may not delete cloud-stored data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when RecallNote's features or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact CEglobal through our website: https://ceglobal.org/.
