How to Prepare a Multi-Photo Post on Your iPhone

Published 2026-11-03

Start by Selecting Your Photos

Creating a multi-photo post on your iPhone begins with choosing the images that best tell your story or showcase your moment. Open the Photos app to browse through recent snaps or albums. Think about the theme or message you want to convey, and select multiple photos that complement each other. You can select photos by tapping "Select" in the upper corner and then tapping the images you want to include.

Add Context with Individual Notes

Once you have your photos, adding captions or notes helps provide context. Not all platforms let you add unique captions to each photo in a multi-photo post, so it’s useful to prepare descriptions beforehand. Apps like PicPost EZ offer a feature where you can attach individual text notes to each photo. This way, your message stays organized, and you can keep track of what you want to share with each image.

For example, imagine you’re sharing pictures from a weekend hiking trip. You might add notes such as "Starting the trail early," "View from the summit," and "Relaxing by the lake." These notes help you remember the story you want to tell when crafting your social media post.

Combine Notes for Easy Sharing

If you plan to post your photos on platforms like Instagram or Facebook, where captions accompany the entire album rather than individual photos, consolidating your notes is helpful. With PicPost EZ, you can select all your photos and combine their individual notes into one coherent block of text. This saves you from having to rewrite captions and ensures your combined message flows well.

After combining the notes, simply copy them to your clipboard and paste them into your social media post. This approach gives you precise control over your message and maintains a natural, engaging tone.

Sharing Your Multi-Photo Post

Finally, after preparing your photos and captions, it’s time to share. If you’re posting to messaging apps or Threads, PicPost EZ allows sharing photos along with the combined notes directly to these platforms, which streamlines the process and ensures your post includes all the context you’ve crafted.

For platforms like Instagram and Facebook, upload your selected photos through the app’s interface, paste the combined notes into the caption box, and then post. This manual step ensures that your captions stay exactly as you want them and that each photo is shown in the order you selected.

Key Tips for an Effective Multi-Photo Post

  • Plan your story: Choose photos that contribute to a clear narrative or theme.
  • Use notes to add detail: Captions make images meaningful and engaging.
  • Consolidate captions: Tools like PicPost EZ can help combine notes for a cleaner post.
  • Review before posting: Make sure the order and caption flow support your intended message.

By following these straightforward steps and utilizing helpful tools like PicPost EZ, you can create compelling multi-photo posts on your iPhone that capture attention and clearly communicate your story.
