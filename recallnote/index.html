Phone & Tablet Sync on Android

RecallNote can synchronize review cards and PDF memos between your Android phone and tablet through Google Drive.

  1. Open RecallNote on your phone and select a Google account.
  2. Open RecallNote on your tablet and select the same Google account.
  3. Your review cards and PDF memos will then be synchronized between the two devices through Google Drive.

After selecting the account once, RecallNote connects automatically on future launches.

When viewing a PDF, tap the memo switch button to alternate between PDF memos created on your phone and tablet.

iPhone, iPad & Mac Sync

RecallNote can sync your study data across iPhone, iPad and Mac through iCloud.

  1. Sign in to the same Apple Account on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  2. Open RecallNote on each device.
  3. Your RecallNote data will sync automatically through iCloud.

No separate RecallNote account is required.

PDF memos are optimized for each device: you can write handwritten memos on iPhone and iPad, and use keyboard-based memos on Mac. You can also switch between device memos to view notes created on your other devices.
